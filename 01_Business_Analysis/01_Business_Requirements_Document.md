
# Solar360 – Business Requirements Document

## 1. Project Information

Project Name: Solar360
Project Type: Product & Business Analysis
Industry: Solar Energy
Document Version: 1.0
Document Status: Initial Draft
Prepared By: Junior Product Analyst
Company: Solar360 Energy Solutions (Fictional)

## 2. Business Background

Solar360 Energy Solutions provides residential and
commercial rooftop solar installation services.

The company manages customer enquiries, site inspections,
quotations, installation scheduling and customer support.

Currently, business teams face challenges in tracking
customer information and operational activities.

## 3. Problem Statement

The organization faces operational inefficiencies due to
fragmented customer information, manual lead tracking,
delayed site inspections, installation scheduling issues
and limited visibility into customer support activities.

A centralized management platform is proposed to improve
operational visibility and streamline business workflows.

## 4. Business Objectives

1. Centralize customer and lead information.
2. Improve lead assignment and follow-up tracking.
3. Monitor site inspection activities.
4. Track installation progress.
5. Manage customer complaints and support tickets.
6. Provide management with performance reports.

## 5. Stakeholders

- Customers
- Sales Executives
- Site Engineers
- Operations Managers
- Support Executives
- Product Manager
- Development Team

## 6. Project Scope

### In Scope
- Customer registration
- Lead management
- Lead assignment
- Follow-up tracking
- Site inspection management
- Installation scheduling
- Support ticket management
- Product performance reporting

### Out of Scope
- Solar panel manufacturing
- Physical installation activities
- Payment gateway integration
- Electricity grid infrastructure

## 7. Business Problems

BP01 - Customer enquiries are not tracked centrally.
BP02 - Site inspections experience scheduling delays.
BP03 - Customer records may be incomplete.
BP04 - Installation schedules lack visibility.
BP05 - Customer complaints are not tracked centrally.
BP06 - Management lacks consolidated performance reports.

## 8. Proposed Business Solution

Develop a centralized Solar360 Customer and Installation
Management Platform to manage customer information,
sales activities, inspections, installations and support.

## 9. Success Metrics

- Lead Conversion Rate
- Inspection Completion Rate
- On-Time Installation Rate
- Average Installation Time
- Support Ticket Resolution Time
- Customer Satisfaction Score

## 10. Assumptions and Constraints

- All project data will be synthetic.
- The platform is a portfolio case study.
- Initial implementation will use free tools.
- Requirements will be validated against the
  simulated business scenario.
- No real customer or company confidential data
  will be used.
