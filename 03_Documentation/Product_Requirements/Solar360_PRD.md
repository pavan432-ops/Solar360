
# Solar360 – Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

Product Name: Solar360
Company: Solar360 Energy Solutions (Fictional)
Product Type: Customer and Installation Management Platform
Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

Solar360 is a centralized platform designed to help solar
energy businesses manage customer enquiries, sales leads,
site inspections, installations and customer support.

## 2. Product Vision

To provide a centralized and transparent platform that
simplifies solar customer management and improves
operational efficiency.

## 3. Target Users

- Sales Executives
- Site Engineers
- Operations Managers
- Support Executives
- Customers
- Administrators

## 4. User Problems

- Customer enquiries are tracked in separate Excel sheets.
- Duplicate customer contacts may occur.
- Sales follow-ups may be missed.
- Site inspection schedules lack visibility.
- Installation progress is difficult to monitor.
- Customer complaints are tracked separately.
- Management lacks consolidated reports.

## 5. Product Goals

1. Centralize customer and lead information.
2. Improve lead assignment and follow-up visibility.
3. Simplify site inspection scheduling.
4. Track installation progress.
5. Centralize customer support activities.
6. Provide management performance dashboards.

## 6. Product Scope

### MVP – Version 1.0

- Customer registration
- Lead creation and management
- Lead assignment
- Follow-up tracking
- Site inspection scheduling
- Installation status tracking
- Support ticket management
- Basic management reporting

### Future Enhancements

- Automated WhatsApp notifications
- Mobile application
- Advanced sales analytics
- AI-based lead scoring
- Customer self-service portal

## 7. Core Product Modules

### Module 1 – Customer Management

Users can register, view and update customer information.

### Module 2 – Lead Management

Sales executives can create leads, assign ownership,
record follow-ups and update lead statuses.

### Module 3 – Site Inspection

Authorized users can schedule inspections and
track inspection outcomes.

### Module 4 – Installation Management

Operations users can schedule installations and
monitor progress and delays.

### Module 5 – Customer Support

Support executives can create and resolve
customer support tickets.

### Module 6 – Analytics Dashboard

Managers can monitor lead conversion, inspections,
installation performance and support resolution.

## 8. Key Product Metrics

- Lead Conversion Rate
- Pending Follow-up Count
- Inspection Completion Rate
- On-Time Installation Rate
- Average Installation Duration
- Support Ticket Resolution Time
- Customer Satisfaction Score

## 9. Product Success Criteria

- Users can manage customer and lead records centrally.
- Lead ownership is visible.
- Overdue follow-ups can be identified.
- Inspection and installation statuses are trackable.
- Managers can access consolidated performance reports.

## 10. Assumptions and Constraints

- This is a fictional portfolio project.
- All datasets will be synthetic.
- The first release will focus on core workflows.
- Free development tools will be used.
- Product requirements are draft and pending validation.

## 11. Release Plan

Release 1.0: Core customer, lead and operations management.
Release 1.1: Improved reporting and notifications.
Release 2.0: Advanced analytics and customer portal.
